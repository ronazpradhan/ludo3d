Jutpatti

Make every card a pair · 2–6 players
How to play

Everyone gets an odd number of cards. The card turned face up in the middle picks the Joker: every card one rank higher is a Joker and pairs with anything.

On your turn drag a card from the stock or the discard pile into your hand, then drag one card back onto the table.

When all your cards make pairs right after you draw, a Show button lights up — tap it to win. Dragging cards onto each other to keep track is optional.
