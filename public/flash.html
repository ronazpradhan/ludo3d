How to play

A round is 10 games. Everyone starts the round with Rs 1000 — use it wisely, it only resets next round. The richest player after 10 games wins the round.

Each game everyone puts in the boot and gets 3 cards face down. Play blind (cheaper bets) or tap your cards to see them. On your turn: Chaal (bet), Raise, Pack (fold), Show (when two are left) or Side show with the previous player.

Best to worst: Trail (three of a kind) · Pure sequence · Sequence · Color · Pair · High card.

Sapati: short on chips? Borrow from a player (or the bank if nobody lends). It's all paid back at the end of the round, before the winner is decided.

Play money only — chips have no real-world value.
