Dhumbal

Jhyap · get your hand low · 2–5 players
How to play

Everyone gets 5 cards. Points: A = 1, 2–10 face value, J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13. Get your hand as low as you can.

On your turn throw first — one card, a set (2+ of the same rank) or a run (3+ in a row, same suit) — then draw: the top of the deck or one of the cards the previous player threw.

At the start of your turn, with 10 points or less, call Jhyap! Everyone shows. Lowest hand? Everyone pays you their points. But if someone has the same or less, they win and you pay everyone's points to them.

Points are play money only — no real-world value.
